USABILITY TEST RESULTS

Observations

FREE DRAW

During user testing, many of the participants took the “draw” prompt and ran with it, drawing whatever they pleased. That was not my intended purpose for this project; users are to draw their perception of the future. Thus, I plan to include an overarching prompt either on the title page or the drawing interface, indicating to users that they should draw something related to their idea of the future.

INTERFACE

In testing, I found users looking for an eraser tool, and confused about the custom color picker, often avoiding the swatch completely in favor of the other, established colors. I will add an eraser tool, a paint bucket to fill the background, and better styling to the color picker to indicate that users can create custom colors not offered in the standard palette.

GALLERY NAV

Users expressed the desire to view the gallery without having to submit a drawing. I will add a button to the drawing interface that takes them directly to the gallery.


Next Steps

I need to make my design a little more intuitive. Adding more directives, cleaning up my buttons, and refining my drawing interface will make the user experience smoother. Most users had an easy time navigating my project, but they vocalized that they wished it were easier to go between the pages, so I’ll be sure to add more navigation features that make the site easier to explore. In testing, it sounded like the prompts I provided helped users come up with ideas, and the fact that drawing on a computer is a bit unwieldy caused them to stop and think more about the future and what they were drawing.
